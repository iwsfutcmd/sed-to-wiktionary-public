    Forms follow SED notation: V an unspecified vowel, / an alternation, (…) optional material, ◊ the variant with affricates, // labialised alternant. Alternate forms are separated by commas. “Wiktionary spelling” converts ŝ→ś, ṣ̂→ṣ́, ṯ̣→ṱ, γ→ġ, ḳ→q on the first variant; the “Wiktionary” button opens the matching page when one exists, otherwise the would-be Proto-Semitic title.
