==Arabic==

===Etymology 1===
{{ar-rootbox|ب ق ق}}
From {{der|ar|sem-pro|*baḳḳ-|t=gnat}}, ultimately stemming from {{der|ar|afa-pro|*ba(n)ḳ-|t=troublesome insect}}, with cognates in {{cog|cdc-wst|-}}, {{cog|fie|ḅakyáà-ŋ|t=scorpion}}, and to {{cog|cus|-}}, {{cog|hdy|benḳ-aččo|t=tick}}. {{unc|ar}} if cognate to or continuing {{der|ar|arc|בָּקָּא}} / {{m|syc|ܒܳܩܳܐ|t=gnat|tr=bāqā, baqqā}}, already found in lexical list [[w:Urra%3Dhubullu|ḪAR-ra=''ḫubullu'']] as {{der|ar|akk|𒁀𒀝𒄣|tr=ba-aq-qu|ts=baqqu, bāqu|t=gnat}}, also in diminutive forms {{m|akk|𒁍𒂵𒆪𒌝|tr=bu-qa-qu-um|ts=buqāqu}}, and {{m|akk|𒁀𒀝𒁀𒀝𒄣
|tr=ba-aq-ba-aq-qu|ts=baqbaqqu|}}; possibly inherited separately if {{cog|hss|beqqét}} and {{m|hss|beqáyt|t=bug}} are not borrowed from Arabic, but attest a {{noncog|sem-sar|-}} form.
